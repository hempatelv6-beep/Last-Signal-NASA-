# Team Roles (5 members)

Everyone writes, everyone tests, and everyone fact-checks someone else's work. Roles just say who is **responsible**.

| Role | Main job | Deliverables | Works on |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Team Lead + Integrator** | Keeps the plan on track and owns the repo and deployment | Repo + GitHub Pages live; `stories.json` merged; final submission | Block 1 setup, merging, submission form, keeping time |
| **2. Story Engine Developer** | Builds the core story player | `story.html`, `story.js` (scenes → decision → reveal → learned) | The main feature, then the Apollo weight mini-choice |
| **3. Pages & Design Developer** | Makes it look good and work on phones | `index.html`, `map.html`, `cards.html`, `style.css`, Fate Cards | Home, map, cards, visual style, mobile testing |
| **4. Head Writer (Moon)** | Writes 3 stories in kid-friendly language | Apollo 15, Surveyor 3, ALSEP stories with NASA links | Story text, decision options, "What we learned"; fact-checks the Mars stories |
| **5. Science Lead + Writer (Mars) + Media** | Accuracy and images | Spirit, Phoenix, Ingenuity stories; all images + credits; demo video | Images (WebP), credits file, fact-checks the Moon stories, records the video |

## Rules that keep it fair and smooth
- **Fact-check swap:** #4 checks #5's facts and #5 checks #4's. Nobody approves their own facts.
- **One file, one owner** during building, to avoid Git conflicts.
- **Check-in every 3 hours** (5 minutes): what's done, what's blocked, what's next.
- **Feature freeze** at the end of Block 4. After that, only fixes and polish.
- **AI log:** each person adds one line to `AI-USE.md` whenever AI helped.
- Everyone tests the site on their own phone before submitting.

## If someone finishes early
Help in this order: fact-checking → testing with outsiders → French translation → read-aloud button.
